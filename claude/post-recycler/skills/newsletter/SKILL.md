# newsletter

## newsletter.md

- Line 1: `Objet : ...` (or `Subject: ...` in English). 60 characters at most, the result or the question it answers. No clickbait the body does not pay off.
- Body, 80 to 250 words:
  - the context in 1 or 2 sentences (why you did it);
  - what you found, as a short list with the source's numbers;
  - what the reader can do with it this week;
  - one link or one reply request. Email readers can reply: "reply with X" replaces "comment X".

## Rules

Only facts, numbers and links from the source. No long or medium dash, no hashtag. Run `fact-guard` after writing.
