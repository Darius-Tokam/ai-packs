# quote-card

## quote-card.md

One sentence of 20 words at most, the line of the source someone would screenshot. Prefer a sentence with a number or a contrast that is in the source. No attribution line: the design adds the name.

## story.md

3 to 5 screens separated by `---`, 15 words per screen at most:
1. the hook (the result);
2. and 3. the proof, one number per screen;
4. the call to action of the source (keyword or link sticker).

## Rules

Only facts and numbers from the source. No long or medium dash, no hashtag. Run `fact-guard` after writing.
