# carousel

## carousel.md

Slides separated by a line `---`. 5 to 10 slides, 30 words per slide at most.

1. **Cover**: the result in 8 words or fewer, plus who it is for.
2. **Problem**: the situation the reader knows, with the source's detail.
3. to n-2. **One item per slide**: title in 3 to 5 words, then the fact or number from the source.
n-1. **Result**: the verdict or the before/after.
n. **Action**: the call to action of the source, same keyword, same link.

## Rules

- A slide that needs more than 30 words is two slides.
- Only facts and numbers from the source. No hashtag, no long or medium dash.
- Write a short visual note in brackets only if it helps the designer, e.g. `[capture du verdict]`. It counts in the word limit, keep it short.
- Run `fact-guard` after writing.
