# insta-caption

## instagram.md

- First line: the result, short enough to show before Instagram cuts the caption. No greeting.
- Body: 3 to 6 short lines, one fact each, taken from the source.
- Call to action: the source's (comment a keyword, link in bio). Never promise a resource the source does not offer.
- Up to 5 hashtags on the last line, specific to the topic (no #love, #instagood).
- 2 200 characters at most.

## Rules

Only facts and numbers from the source. No long or medium dash. Run `fact-guard` after writing.
