# video-script

## video-script.md

- Spoken lines are plain text. On-screen text and camera notes are on their own lines in brackets: `[texte : 8/8]`, `[plan : écran du terminal]`. Bracket lines are not spoken.
- 55 to 90 spoken words: about 30 seconds at a calm pace.
- 0 to 3 s: the result, said in one sentence, with its number on screen.
- 3 to 20 s: how, in 3 beats at most, each with its proof.
- 20 to 30 s: the call to action of the source.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, no parentheses, numbers said the way people say them.

## Rules

Only facts and numbers from the source. No long or medium dash, no hashtag. Run `fact-guard` after writing.
